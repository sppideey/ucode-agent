# Contributing to ucode

Thank you for helping. Two things make a contribution one ucode can take:

1. **Sign off every commit:** `git commit -s`. The `Signed-off-by:` line says
   you agree to the [Developer Certificate of Origin 1.1](https://developercertificate.org):
   that you wrote the change, or have the right to submit it.
2. **What you contribute is licensed under the MIT License** (MIT No
   Attribution in `templates/` and `skills/`). ucode as a whole is released
   under the GNU AGPL v3 with additional terms (see [NOTICE](NOTICE)), and its
   code is also used in other projects by its author; contributions under MIT
   can go into all of them. You keep the copyright to what you wrote.

Before a pull request: `npm test` passes, and one change per pull request.

Keep the credit "made and tested by om dixit" where it is — the licence
requires it ([NOTICE](NOTICE), term 1).
